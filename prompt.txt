Welcome to the Bateel Boutique Brand Guidelines manual.  
This document will explain how to construct communications that are visually consistent, effective and above all – clearly distinctive of our brand.

We have illustrated each individual element of our brand, outlined guides on application and shown how to bring it to life using our carefully crafted range of brand assets.

How to Use This Document:

1. Understanding our Brand  
   The 'About us' section provides key information regarding the strategy, messaging and values that drive us as a company.

2. Our Brand Identity  
   The brand identity section is an overview of all aspects of our visual language. It is imperative that these assets are used correctly.

3. Application  
   Our brand assets are designed to be applied across a range of visual tools and applications. When used correctly, the brand identity should appear clear and consistent across all touchpoints.

About Bateel chunk



Bateel is a gourmet food business with a distinct difference.  
As both a cultivator and curator of fine food, Bateel travels the world to bring exceptional experiences to an appreciative audience.

Our aim is to delight and educate, through a culinary journey of discovery.

We operate with a Branded House architecture.  
All products and services are centrally branded under Bateel, creating stronger customer relationships and brand recognition.  
This leads to greater trust, ease of launching new products, and an elevated perception of our core values.

Our Brand Architecture

Bateel is a Branded House.

Given that all products and services are centrally branded, recognition and customer loyalty tend to bubble up to the parent company.

In turn, this creates a stronger customer relationship that extends beyond the value of the product, to an elevated respect for the inherent values of the company.

This can equate to greater trust and an easier sell of new or related products, even extensions.

Our Logo

Our logo is the most important element of our visual identity.  
It is the visual embodiment of our brand that people instantly come to recognise and associate with excellence.

We use the Bateel core logo for our Corporate and Boutique brand identity, and it should be used in all cases for collateral, communications and packaging — there are no exceptions.

The logo consists of Arabic and English text elements, carefully proportioned and crafted.

It must never be modified, reconstructed, or misused in any way. The integrity of the logo must be preserved across all applications.

image:
![Logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/logo.png)


---

LOGO: PROPORTION

Logo: Proportion

Our logo has been crafted with a primary focus on Arabic and a smaller proportion of the English translation.

We ask you not to deconstruct, change, or modify any elements in our logo.  
All parts and proportions have been carefully considered.

The height of the Arabic text is used as the rule for the x-height in layout and spacing.

image:
![LOGO PROPORTION](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/LOGO%20PROPORTION.png)

📘 Chunk: Logo – Clearance Space
Logo: Clearance Space

We have defined an exclusion zone that stops other graphic elements from interfering with the Bateel logo.

To ensure that the logo is always seen clearly across all materials, we have defined two versions of clearance space:

1. Signage Application  
   The space between the Arabic and English elements defines the clearance zone.  
   This guide ensures maximum visibility and optimal placement on storefronts.
   ![1 Signage Application](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/1%20Signage%20Application.png)

2. Printed Collateral Application  
   The height of the Arabic motif is used as the x-height to determine the minimum clearance around the logo.
![2 Printed Collateral Application](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/2%20Printed%20Collateral%20Application.png)

Logo – Positioning

Logo: Positioning

The primary positioning of the Bateel logo for communications is in the top-right or bottom-right corner.

For smaller formats, such as business cards, the logo can be positioned centrally.  
This principle can also be applied for packaging.

1. Stationery & Communications  
   The Bateel logo should be placed in the corners on items like letterheads and compliment slips, and centered on business cards.
![Logo Positioning 1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/logo%20positioning%201.png)

2. Packaging  
   Packaging formats can adopt a centred principle for logo positioning to maintain clarity and elegance.

![Logo Positioning 2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/logo%20positioning%202.png)

---

LOGO: SIZING

Logo: Sizing

The logo must always be easily distinguished and appear clear and consistent.

As long as the clearance space rule is adhered to, the logo can be used as large as desired.

We have set a minimum size of 12mm in height to ensure legibility and brand consistency, especially in print and small-scale applications.

![Logo Sizing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yasserkh2/images/main/logo%20sizing%20.png)
